
--- UNIVERSITY STUDENT HANDBOOK ---
SECTION 1: EXAM SCHEDULE
End-semester examinations commence on December 1st every academic year. Hall tickets are available 7 days prior on the student portal.

SECTION 2: ADMISSION PROCEDURES
Admissions for postgraduate courses require a minimum 60% aggregate score in undergraduate degree and valid national entrance exam percentile.

SECTION 3: HOSTEL RULES
Curfew time for all hostel residents is 10:00 PM. Visitors are allowed in the common lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM only.
